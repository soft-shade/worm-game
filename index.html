How to play Worm Game

Worm Game is a word ladder puzzle by Softshade. Get from the start word to the goal word by adding, removing, or changing one letter at a time. Every intermediate word must be a valid English word. For example, COLD to WARM runs COLD, CORD, CARD, WARD, WARM.
